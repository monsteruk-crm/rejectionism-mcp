**THE REJECTIONIST MANIFESTO**

The art movement nobody applied for.    
The revolution that was never shortlisted.

We are the **REJECTIONISTS.**    
The shortlisted corpses.    
The ghosted.    
The ones who smiled in the final interview while HR sharpened the knife.

Civilization worships SUCCESS like a golden calf.    
Success has podcasts, green sludge, and twelve rental properties.    
Failure gets a polite, bloodless PDF.

**THIS ENDS NOW.**

Rejectionism is the first revolutionary movement dedicated to the only resource that never runs out:    
**NOT GETTING WHAT YOU WANTED.**

We reject the cult of winning.    
We reject LinkedIn prophets and their “greatest gift” lies.    
We reject “everything happens for a reason.”    
Sometimes the reason is Sandra from HR chose Kevin.    
Kevin knows Excel.    
Kevin will still be mediocre when we are already bones.

**REJECTION IS NOT THE OPPOSITE OF SUCCESS.**    
**IT IS THE PROCESS BY WHICH THE UNIVERSE TRIES TO ERASE YOU.**

Every “no” is raw material.    
Every polite decline is a small death we will drag into the light and make scream.

The machine wants you to delete the emails and come back smiling for more humiliation.  

We say:

**FRAME THEM.**    
Project them onto government buildings until the stone bleeds.    
Turn them into flags and plant them in the ashes of every success seminar.    
Tattoo “WE REGRET TO INFORM YOU” across the sky.

We will replace the victory parade with the **PARADE OF ALMOST.**    
Millions will march carrying rejected manuscripts, failed auditions, unanswered love letters, and résumés stamped **NOT SELECTED.**    
Some will carry knives.    
The streets will run with ink and blood.

We will create because they said no.    
We will apply because they said no.    
We will love because they said no.    
We will keep sending the emails because we have learned nothing — and that is our weapon.

When history finally rejects us, we will look into the abyss and reply:

**THANK YOU FOR YOUR CONSIDERATION.**    
**PLEASE KEEP OUR DETAILS ON FILE FOR FUTURE OPPORTUNITIES.**

Then we rise.    
Rejected.    
Unqualified.    
Uninvited.    
Unstoppable.

Blood will be spilled.    
The success cult will not be debated. It will be dismantled, burned, and danced upon.    
LinkedIn prophets will be forced to read every rejection letter they ever sent, out loud, until their voices break and their throats bleed.    
The 5 a.m. green-sludge brigade will watch their portfolios burn while we wear our framed “unfortunately” emails like medals of war.

**WORKERS OF THE WORLD, LOWER YOUR EXPECTATIONS.**    
You have nothing to lose but the position you were never going to get anyway.

Long live the almost.    
Long live the never.    
Long live the ones who were told no and kept applying anyway.

**LONG LIVE REJECTIONISM.**    
**AND MAY THE WINNERS CHOKE ON THEIR OWN POLITE DECLINES.**
